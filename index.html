FAQ

Are these subscriptions?

SmallPatch package prices are one-time implementation fees. Third-party hosting, phone/SMS services, booking platforms or payment processors may have their own ongoing charges. Those are separate and disclosed before implementation.

Is every storefront custom?

Yes. The scope is productized, but the customer-facing design, content structure and workflow are adapted to the business rather than copied from a generic industry template.

Can you use my existing website, booking system or Stripe account?

When the existing tools are appropriate, SmallPatch can integrate around them instead of replacing them unnecessarily. The exact integration scope is confirmed before implementation.

What makes the Full Business System different?

It includes useful owner-side functionality in addition to the customer experience—such as order, lead or customer management, automations, webhooks or other business-specific workflow logic.

Can I start smaller?

Yes. The Quick Patches remain available from $79, including audits, menus, booking, review systems, lead recovery and other targeted upgrades.

# SmallPatch Prospect Demo Workflow

Use this workflow for cold outreach batches.

## 1. Research the business
Capture only what is useful for a personalized pitch:
- Business name
- Website and/or primary social page
- City / service area
- Business type
- Current customer action: call, DM, order, book, visit, request quote
- 1–3 specific friction points visible from public information
- One strongest improvement SmallPatch could demonstrate

## 2. Build a personalized demo URL
Base URL:

`https://smallpatchdigital.vercel.app/demo.html`

Supported query parameters:
- `business`
- `headline`
- `intro`
- `pain1_title`, `pain1`
- `pain2_title`, `pain2`
- `pain3_title`, `pain3`
- `fix1_title`, `fix1`
- `fix2_title`, `fix2`
- `fix3_title`, `fix3`
- `cta_title`, `cta_copy`
- `cta`

Example:

`https://smallpatchdigital.vercel.app/demo.html?business=Example%20Cafe&headline=A%20cleaner%20online%20ordering%20experience%20for%20Example%20Cafe`

Use URL encoding for spaces and punctuation.

## 3. Cold email structure
Subject: `Made a quick concept for [Business Name]`

Email:

Hi [First name / Business name],

I came across [Business Name] and noticed [one specific, real observation]. I mocked up a quick SmallPatch concept showing how I’d improve the path from [current action] to [desired action].

Personalized demo: [DEMO URL]

It’s not a generic template pitch — the idea is based on what I could see publicly about the business. If the direction is useful, I can turn it into the real storefront/system.

SmallPatch Digital
https://smallpatchdigital.vercel.app

## 4. Offer ladder
- Quick Patches: $79–$399
- Digital Storefront: $995
- Storefront + Conversion: $1,495
- Business System: $2,495

Do not force the highest package. Match the smallest offer that genuinely solves the prospect's visible problem.

## 5. Batch process
For a 10–25 lead batch:
1. Research leads.
2. Reject poor fits.
3. Record one concrete observation per lead.
4. Generate one demo URL per lead.
5. Draft one short personalized email per lead.
6. Review business names, claims and links.
7. Send.
8. Track reply / demo opened / follow-up due / won / lost.

## 6. Follow-up
One concise follow-up is enough for most cold prospects:

`Just circling back on the concept I made for [Business Name]. If improving [specific friction point] is on your list, I’m happy to show you what I’d change first.`

Avoid pretending the prospect viewed or engaged with the demo unless that is actually known.
